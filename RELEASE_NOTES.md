## SRM Recorder 1.4.0: Screen Recorder has a new name

**Screen Recorder is now SRM Recorder.** It's the same free, tiny screen recorder for Mac, with a name that's easy to find.

### What's new
- New name everywhere: in the app, the menu bar, the About window and in Applications. When you update from inside the app, your copy is renamed to **SRM Recorder** automatically.
- Your settings, your recordings and your Screen Recording permission all stay as they are.

### Features
- Record the full screen, a single window or any area you select
- Record **system audio** and/or your **microphone**. Each can be turned on or off, and both are mixed into one track.
- A red **recording frame** shows exactly what is being recorded. A floating bar shows the timer, Pause and Stop. Neither appears in the video.
- Pause and Resume, a countdown, and global shortcuts (⌥⌘R start/stop, ⌥⌘P pause/resume)
- Menu bar timer, recordings library, MP4/MOV, H.264/HEVC, up to 60 fps
- Automatic updates

### Install
You don't need to install anything else. Everything the app uses is already part of macOS.

1. Download **SRMRecorder-1.4.0.dmg**, open it and drag **SRM Recorder** into **Applications**.
2. The first time you open it, right-click the app and choose **Open**. On macOS 15 and later, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Allow **Screen Recording** when asked, then click **Quit & Reopen** in the app.
